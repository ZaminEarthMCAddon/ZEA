The textures in this directory are part of ZEA's market viewer — the
JEI-style screen that opens over the inventory when an item is clicked
in the item browser.

All of these files — panel.png, button.png, button_hover.png,
button_pressed.png, button_disabled.png, icons/arrow_next.png,
icons/arrow_previous.png, tab_selected.png, tab_unselected.png — are
copied unmodified from the JustEnoughItems repository
(https://github.com/mezz/JustEnoughItems, formerly
assets/jei/textures/gui/sprites/, renamed), copyright the JEI
contributors (mezz and the JustEnoughItems contributors), distributed
under the MIT License. ZEA is not endorsed by or affiliated with the
JEI project.
